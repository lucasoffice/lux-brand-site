Calculation basis and what changed

Existing elevation = 1,000 − raw laser reading. The earlier map used 1,016 mm, so all existing and repair-top elevations are now 16 mm lower. Raw measurements, repair depths and locations are unchanged.

Door substrate = 20 − 12.5 = +7.5 mm. The red level area, both portals and the connected BOH door triangle use +21 mm substrate. This is 16 mm higher physically than the former +21 mm target on the old datum.

Front and lower-right slope targets retain their previous spatial progression: fraction = (old target +17.1125) ÷ 38.1125, bounded to 0–1; revised target = 7.5 + 13.5 × fraction. The existing map’s front prepared-edge line is used as the door-side endpoint pending field confirmation of the exact door line. BOH feather targets interpolate between the corrected surrounding slab and the +21 mm triangle using the previous taper fractions. The far-left BOH retains existing elevations with local repairs.

Fill = max(target − repaired base, 0); grind = max(repaired base − target, 0). At a defect, repair to the assumed surrounding slab first, then add the finish preparation. There is no blanket minimum topping. Wood finish targets are substrate +12.5 mm in the proposed wood areas; no wood thickness is deducted from the bluestone vestibule or assigned to retained BOH.

Coordinates, zones and taper progression come from the November 9 vendor issue. Targets inherited to 0.001 mm are computational values, not setting-out tolerances. This revision does not reuse the old material bag counts or boundary-integrated quantities; those require a revised takeoff before purchasing. Four-foot survey spacing does not establish local surface flatness.
