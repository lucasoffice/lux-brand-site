Understanding
01

Schedule was accelerated two weeks by executive order.

The original timeline was a September 1 start with a November 1 opening. Suit Supply revised to August 17 and October 16, 2026, tear-down April 1, 2027. General conditions are priced for nine weeks, not the twelve-week baseline.

02

Millwork supply is owner-carried and LUX installs.

Per Edmund Spencer's July 6 direction and Hélène Tran's July 9 email, LUX prices the full scope outside millwork supply. Receipt and installation of 30 furniture pieces, 22 built-ins and 18 Suitwave units is priced at $40,000 as its own line.

03

Two drawing sets govern together.

The HBC Issue for Permit, LL Review, Bid Set dated 07/01/2026 governs for permit and construction. The Puur+ Complete Set 12-06 supplements it for design intent, finishes and millwork. Where they disagree the conflict was written into the RFI register.

04

Storefront repaint conflicts with the facade intent on sheet 300.

The July 28 request for a full black exterior repaint including the wood door conflicts with Puur+ sheet 300, which directs KEEP EXISTING MARBLE and KEEP EXISTING BRASS LINE. No dimensioned storefront elevation was issued, so LUX scaled 34'-7" of frontage at about 10'-2" floor to head and marked the repaint as an allowance to be trued at a painter site walk.

05

Ecoline flooring requires a full sand to bare wood.

Saicos Ecoline is a penetrating oil that requires bare wood, so the full sand across about 3,900 SF is a requirement of the specified product rather than an add. The allowance moved from $14,500 to $30,000 for that reason. Confirmation is needed that the intent is Ecoline oil and not a literal black floor paint.

06

Tenant area and LED mirror counts were inconsistent across documents.

Documents showed 5,600, 5,644 and 5,668 SF. Hélène Tran confirmed 5,644 SF as authoritative for permits and that figure is used throughout. LED mirror units remain open at 16 per Plan 100 against 14 per the Sourcing Overview; the price carries 16 and the delta returns as a credit.

07

Union job site confirmed.

The Non-Union reference in the v1.5 proposal was a clerical error. All trade pricing was already union-based, so the correction carries no price change in v1.7. Every subcontractor number behind this lump sum was quoted on union rates.

08

NJ tax treatment is a capital improvement.

Labor is untaxed as a capital improvement, with an ST-8 Certificate of Capital Improvement executed by Suit Supply at award. A 6.625 percent provision of $3,137.60 is carried on estimated material portions of trades that did not price sales tax within their own scope.

How This Number Was Built

Fifteen RFIs ranked by cost impact replaced ten padded allowance lines.

LUX issued a 15-item RFI register ordered by dollar exposure so Hélène Tran and Matthew Dresser could close the high-dollar items before buyout. Landlord pass-throughs carry no markup and mirror sizes are set by joint field verification before glass is cut.

01

Ask the questions instead of padding the allowances.

Uncertainty was written into a 15-item RFI register ranked by cost impact rather than buried in fat allowance lines. The register puts the storefront repaint extent, the flooring intent, the mirror count and the sign program at the top so the owner and architect can close the expensive items first and drive the number down at buyout.

02

Every uncertain line is a clearly marked allowance.

Ten lines are named: painting and wallcovering installation, storefront repaint, flooring prep and application, concrete curb cut, dumpster volume, molding and door supply, display-window plinths, the Level-5 drywall upgrade, sign permit and engineering, and the mall sprinkler shutoff fee. All ten are trued to final subcontractor pricing by change proposal.

03

Nine-week union build to a fixed opening date.

Mobilization is August 17 and the store opens October 16, 2026, with superintendent and project manager carried for nine weeks against union rates and mall access rules. Millwork deliveries are assumed from approximately September 21, and phased deliveries are acceptable so glazing and FF&E can follow in sequence.

04

Joint field verification before glass is cut.

Mirror and glazing sizes are carried per LBCo takeoff, but final sizes are set by joint field verification before any glass is cut. Plinth and mirror heights are set on site for the same reason. Remake risk on 767 SF of glass and 16 LED units is removed by a measurement, not by a contingency.

05

Landlord and pass-through costs at cost, no markup.

The mall sprinkler shutoff and recharge fee is paid by LBCo direct to the mall at cost with zero markup applied. It sits in the price as a $1,500 marked allowance and is trued to the mall's actual invoice, up or down, when the invoice arrives.

06

Trades file their own permits under the LBCo master.

The electrical and fire protection subcontractors file their own NJ permits under the LBCo master permit. Approvals move in parallel instead of serially through a single filing, which matters when the construction window is nine weeks and the opening date is fixed.

07

Owner-supplied material logistics handled as a priced discipline.

Receiving, staging and placement of Miami, Short Hills and US Warehouse goods is carried at $10,000 as its own line. Free-issued goods are inspected on receipt and tracked against the Sourcing Overview, including ADA bench dimension verification on the M.9 fitting-room bench.

The register was issued to lower the number at buyout, not to protect it.

10
Commercial Guardrails

The mall sprinkler shutoff fee passes through at $1,500 with zero markup.

Two commitments govern how money moves on this job. Landlord and mall fees are paid at cost and trued to invoice, and every allowance is named line by line and credited back where it is not spent.

Guardrail 01

Landlord fees passed through at cost with no markup.

The mall sprinkler shutdown and recharge fee is carried as a $1,500 marked allowance. LBCo pays the mall direct at cost, applies zero markup, and trues the line to the mall's actual invoice. If the invoice comes in under $1,500 the difference returns to Suit Supply as a credit; if it comes in over, the delta is documented with the invoice attached rather than absorbed into another trade line. Permit and municipal fees, landlord fees, deposits and utility connection costs sit outside this price entirely.

Guardrail 02

Allowances are named line by line and credited back.

All ten allowance lines are identified in Clarification 16 and trued by change proposal against final subcontractor pricing. The $3,000 Level-5 drywall allowance is credited in full if the drywall subcontractor already carried Level 5 in his base scope at the fitting room partitions. The flooring allowance moved from $14,500 to $30,000 only because Ecoline oil requires bare wood across about 3,900 SF, and it will be trued to the sanding extent Hélène Tran confirms. No contingency sits behind these lines; the uncertainty is on the page where it can be closed.
